# Cookie Policy

**Application:** Booking System
**Last Updated:** March 2026

## Note

The application did not provide a cookie policy at `/cookiepolicy`.
This document proposes a Cookie Policy to improve transparency regarding how cookies are used in the application.

---

# 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device when visiting a website or web application.
They help the system recognize users, maintain sessions, and improve the functionality of the application.

---

# 2. How the Booking System Uses Cookies

The Booking System uses cookies or session-based mechanisms to support core functionality of the application.

Cookies may be used for the following purposes:

* Maintaining user login sessions
* Authenticating users during system usage
* Ensuring secure access to protected areas of the application

These cookies are necessary for the system to function properly.

---

# 3. Types of Cookies Used

## Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for the basic operation of the application.

They allow users to:

* Log in to their accounts
* Access protected features
* Maintain an active session while using the application

Without these cookies, the application would not function correctly.

---

# 4. Third-Party Cookies

The Booking System **does not use third-party cookies** such as advertising or analytics cookies.

All cookies used by the system are related only to the internal functionality of the application.

---

# 5. Managing Cookies

Users can control or delete cookies through their web browser settings.

However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the application, particularly login sessions and access to secured areas.

---

# 6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated in the future if changes occur in how cookies are used within the application.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
